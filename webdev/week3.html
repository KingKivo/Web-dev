Week 3


Games programming

In our third week of games programming, we looked at pointers and arrays. We started off with some examples on the slides and then moved on to actually trying it ourselves. By the end of it we had the knowledge to be able to create and use classes, instances, arrays, create UML flowcharts among other things after learning array memory management on stack pointers.


Website development

Once again similarly to previous weeks, week 3 was going further in depth in regards to the structure and usage of html and css. We took a look at a previous few alumni websites and got a bit more of a grasp of what we are actually supposed to be aiming for when it comes to our final submissions of the website that we submit in week 14. On top of that we also got a pdf uploaded that outlined the requirements we should follow if we want to get a decent grade by the end of the module submission.
